# App Roles

The app has two roles. Their needs differ because a Diner's group shares the bill equally, so fairness means an even split, with shares that differ by at most a cent and add up exactly to the bill. A Bill Organiser has already paid the whole bill, so fairness means each person pays in proportion to what they ordered. Any shortfall comes out of the Organiser's own pocket.

## Diner

A Diner is one person in a group eating out who, when the bill arrives, wants to settle on a fair tip and split the bill evenly between everyone, usually with only a phone in hand. They can enter the receipt subtotal and any tax and service charges, choose a preset or custom tip percentage, enter the number of people, and see the tip amount, the grand total and what each person pays. They must never be shown per-person shares that do not add up exactly to the grand total.

## Bill Organiser

A Bill Organiser is the person who paid the whole bill for a group in which everyone ordered different things, and who now needs to collect a different amount back from each person. They can enter the receipt subtotal, any tax and service charges and a tip percentage, list each person with an optional name and the amount they ordered, add or remove people, and see what each person owes as their order plus a share of tax, charges and tip, along with whether the amounts owed add up to the receipt total. They must never be shown amounts owed that add up to a different figure from the receipt total without a visible warning stating the difference, nor a share of tax, charges and tip for any person that is out of proportion to what they ordered, beyond the single cent needed to make the total add up exactly.

# Jobs To Be Done

Each job describes what the person needs to get done whether or not this app exists. No job mentions the app, a button, a screen or any feature.

## Diner

**J-1 (Diner)**
When the bill arrives at the end of a meal, I want to decide on a tip that is fair for the service, so I can leave a tip without overpaying or looking stingy.

**J-2 (Diner)**
When my group has agreed to share the bill equally, I want to know what each of us owes, with the shares adding up exactly to the bill, so I can pay and leave without anyone arguing over leftover money.

## Bill Organiser

**J-3 (Bill Organiser)**
When I have paid for a group meal on my own, I want to know how much each person owes for what they had, including their fair part of the tax, charges and tip, so I can get my money back without anyone paying for someone else's food.

**J-4 (Bill Organiser)**
When I ask people to pay me back, I want to be sure the amounts I ask for cover the whole bill, so I can avoid being left out of pocket.

**J-5 (Bill Organiser)**
When I have worked out what everyone owes, I want to tell each person their amount quickly, so I can collect the money the same day while everyone remembers the meal.
